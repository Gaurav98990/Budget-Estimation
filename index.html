User Guide

1. Budget & FTE Breakdown

Use Budget-Based to divide budget by blended daily rate and allocate person-days via the three editable ratios. Or use FTE-Based to estimate cost from direct headcount. Rework and contingency reduce usable developer capacity, not the cost already spent.

2. Sprint Sizing & Capacity

Configure sprint length, individual capacity and sprints per release. Calculations follow the formulas shown in the supplied Excel trace: sprint MD/FTE, rounded team sprint velocity, FTE/MD sprint counts and suggested releases.

3. Delivery Roadmap

Choose start date, release number and test-phase lengths, then Build. Toggle the timeline or detailed roadmap. Edit the date or developer staffing for any row, insert extra sprint rows, export CSV for Excel or save and restore all inputs as JSON.

Note: The sample Excel shows 27 estimated development sprints but 20 planned development sprints (five releases × four). This deliberate source rule is retained; a warning appears when planned capacity is below demand. The budget is not increased by adding bug-fix rows.
